# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

tFire is a place to learn and build. We want everyone to take part without being harassed, whatever their age, background, experience, identity or opinions.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Criticise the work, not the person. Give feedback that helps.
- Accept feedback gracefully; assume good intent until shown otherwise.
- Stay on topic in issues and pull requests.

## What we do not allow

- Harassment, insults, or hateful language.
- Publishing private information (addresses, phone numbers, emails) without consent.
- Spam, trolling, or derailing discussions.
- Impersonation.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from the repository and its discussions. The maintainers (Artfical DT) decide.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a violation, report it to **security@artfical.com** or on GitHub (Security tab → Report a violation). All reports are confidential.
